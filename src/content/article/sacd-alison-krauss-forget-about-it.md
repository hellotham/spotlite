**Rounder** · ROUN0465 · hybrid disc · stereo only · PCM recording

**Performance** 4/5 · **Sonics** 3/5

If you are a bluegrass fan, you may well be disappointed by this, for this seems to be an album of soft pop ballads with only hints of country and bluegrass every now and then. I have never heard of Alison Krauss till quite recently, when I discovered one of her songs from this album was featured in the film "The Divine Secrets of the Ya-Ya Sisterhood." Then I saw a copy of this album at the local library and decided to borrow it. After a few listens, I was hooked.

So imagine my pleasure to discover Alison's last three albums are available on Super Audio CD from Rounder Records. The other two, "New Favourites" and "Live" are much more bluegrass oriented, and are Hybrid Multichannel discs. "Forget About It," being one of Rounder Records' first SACD releases is only a Hybrid Stereo disc.

Having listened to the CD version quite a few times, I was surprised that the SACD stereo version is mastered at a lower level (at least -3dB) compared to the CD, and sounds somewhat mellower. The micro-dynamics in Alison's voice comes through more clearly on SACD than on the CD layer, but the CD layer somehow sounds slightly more dynamic.

Also, Alison's voice sounds blander and harsher on the CD layer. Altogether, I was somewhat surprised to find that the CD and SACD tracks result in different listening experiences. I'm not sure which version I prefer.

In summary, a worthwhile buy on SACD but you may find you prefer the CD layer instead.
