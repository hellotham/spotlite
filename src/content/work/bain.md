- Administered an HP 9000 running HP-UX, one of the first commercial UNIX systems in Australia, alongside software development work.
- Secured a licence for ACSnet, the store-and-forward network that preceded the Internet in Australia, and established the link between Bain & Company and the University of Sydney in 1986, giving the firm its own domain name, email and USENET access and making it one of the earliest Australian non-academic sites online.
