- Founder and Junior Janitor of a boutique consultancy spanning business and IT strategy, enterprise architecture, operating models, strategic roadmaps, analytics and data science, digital products and content platforms.
- Author of **AI-dō: The Way of AI**, and developer of **FinvestLens** and **HelloNotes**, two native Apple applications published as free software.
- Successfully delivered multiple consulting engagements, including [Cochlear](https://hellotham.com/spotlite/work/cochlear/), [Transport for NSW](https://hellotham.com/spotlite/work/tfnsw/) and [Broadspectrum](https://hellotham.com/spotlite/work/broadspectrum/).

## Writing

- **AI-dō: The Way of AI, grounded in practice** — a book on working with artificial intelligence in a disciplined, human-centred way. Free to read online, with PDF and ePub editions.

## Applications

- **FinvestLens** — native double-entry accounting for macOS, iPadOS and iOS, built on the rigour of the GnuCash engine. Keeps accounts in local documents, imports multiple formats and generates professional reports. Released as free software under GPLv3.
- **HelloNotes** — a private, local-first Markdown knowledge base for macOS, with wiki-links, backlinks, a graph view, full-text search and on-device intelligence. Works on plain files you control, with no accounts and no tracking.

## Website

- Designed and built hellotham.com, the firm's site and publishing platform for the book and applications, now in its fifth major iteration.
