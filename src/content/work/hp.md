- Joined in October 1995 as a Senior Consultant in the Professional Services Organisation, providing strategic technology planning and IT architecture consulting to HP's customers as part of the Software, Architecture & Methods practice.
- Solution and lead architect for major implementation projects and tender responses, and pre-sales and post-sales consulting on HP products and technologies.
- Team leader for the IT strategy and architecture practice in FY95, responsible for the practice plan including the Hoshin and implementation plans, and for coordinating team meetings.
- Ran the internal mailing list on IT architecture for HP consultants engaged in architecture work.
- Spoke on [electronic commerce and the consumer](/spotlite/article/ecommerce-1997/) at the Finance Industry Reform conference in Sydney in 1997.

## The services

- **IT strategy** — helping customers define a strategy for their IT infrastructure from an understanding of their business objectives.
- **IT assessment** — establishing at a high level how well a customer's current infrastructure supports those objectives, and recommending improvements.
- **Enterprise IT architecture** — the enterprise-wide architectural components needed to support the strategy.
- **IT architecture review** — reviewing an existing architecture and its readiness to adopt emerging technologies, verifying that selected technologies meet architectural guidelines.
- **Transition services** — a plan for moving smoothly to the new environment.

## Boral

- Seconded for seven months as infrastructure architect, reporting to the Group Technology manager of a group with three major business divisions, each with its own IT.
- Defined the Unix server infrastructure strategy, and proposed and initiated a \$2m server upgrade project.
- Developed the business case for consolidating the existing data centres into a common facility, and helped establish service level agreements between centralised Group Technology and the divisions.
- Despite initial reservations from each business unit, the consolidated server and data centre strategy was adopted, for significant operational savings.
